Financial Health

Verify

Marktlink Capital reports rapid growth in investor capital and managed assets, but it is private and does not publish the revenue, profit, cash, or debt information needed to judge its operating financial health.

Finance, in plain language

Subsidiary · improving trend

The firm appears to be growing: it says investors have committed more than €3.5 billion and its managed assets passed €3 billion. But this is not the same as company revenue or profit. Before joining, ask whether the data team and this hire are funded in the operating budget, not only supported by growing investor commitments.
